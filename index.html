About

Emma Harte — Founder

Meet Emma Harte

Emma Harte is a qualified BSc (Hons) Physiotherapist specialising in personalised rehabilitation and community-based support for adults of all ages and abilities.

With years of clinical experience, Emma combines professional expertise with compassionate, motivating care — helping individuals recovering from injury, illness, surgery, or deconditioning to improve their strength, balance, mobility, confidence, and overall wellbeing.

At HomeStep Physio, Emma takes a holistic, person-centred approach, recognising that recovery is about more than physical rehabilitation alone. Through home-based treatment and welcoming movement classes, she creates an inclusive and encouraging environment where individuals feel comfortable, empowered, and motivated throughout their journey.

Emma is passionate about promoting independence, confidence, and healthier movement within both the home and wider community — helping people feel stronger, more capable, and better connected to their wellbeing.

Alongside her physiotherapy practice, Emma is also a trained holistic practitioner, offering complementary therapies including Reiki, cupping therapy, and massage to support relaxation, stress reduction, recovery, and overall wellbeing.

What We Offer

Our Services

Every service is delivered in your own home — tailored to you, at your own pace, with expert guidance every step of the way.

Gentle Strength & Balance

Small-group classes focused on functional strength and balance — keeping you steady, independent, and confident in daily life.

  • For older adults and those in recovery
  • Physiotherapist-led, evidence-based
  • Fall prevention and confidence building
  • Warm, welcoming group environment

Private Home Physiotherapy

One-to-one physiotherapy in the comfort of your home — no travel, no waiting rooms, just expert hands-on care precisely tailored to you.

  • Full assessment and personalised treatment plan
  • Post-surgical and orthopaedic rehabilitation
  • Musculoskeletal pain and injury
  • Mobility, posture, and independence

Holistic Wellbeing & Reiki

An integrative approach blending physiotherapy with Reiki therapy — nurturing body, mind, and spirit in the calm of your own home.

  • Reiki energy therapy at home
  • Stress relief and deep relaxation
  • Complements physiotherapy treatment
  • Restorative holistic wellness
